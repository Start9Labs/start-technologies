**StartWRT 1.2.0 lets you set the Wi-Fi regulatory country, so the router uses the channels and transmit power permitted where you live.**

## Highlights

- **Wi-Fi regulatory country.** `Points of Entry > Wi-Fi > Settings` gains a Country selector. Until you pick one, the router runs on a conservative worldwide subset — 2.4 GHz channels 1–11 and 5 GHz channels 36–48 at 20 dBm — so select your country after updating to unlock the rest of the 5 GHz band and full transmit power. The channel dropdowns now list only the channels the selected country permits. Automatic channel selection avoids radar-detection (DFS) channels; pick one by hand if you want it, and expect the 5 GHz network to take a minute or more to appear each time the router starts.

## Fixes

- **A published domain typed without `https://` now reaches the published service.** While port 443 is published to a device — by a Published Port or a device's hostname routes, as a StartOS server does for its public domains — a plain `http://` connection to the router's public address answers with a redirect to `https://` instead of the router's own web interface. Who may reach port 80 from the WAN side is unchanged: Remote Access still decides that, and with nothing published on 443, port 80 behaves as before.

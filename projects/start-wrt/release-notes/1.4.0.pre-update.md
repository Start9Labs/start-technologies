## ⚠️ Before You Update

If you reach this router's web interface or SSH from outside your local network without a VPN, set up an [Inbound VPN](https://docs.start9.com/start-wrt/inbound-vpn.html) and confirm you can connect through it before you update. After the update, the router accepts those connections only from your local network and (when explicitly authorized) your Inbound VPNs.

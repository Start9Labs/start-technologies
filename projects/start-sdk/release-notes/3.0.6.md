**A patch release: manifests, notifications and container addresses type-check as written for 3.0.4, and action results spell out every field.**

## Highlights

- **Manifests and notifications take what they took in 3.0.4.** Dockerfile images may omit `workdir`, and device filters and notifications may omit their optional fields.
- **`getContainerIp` for the service's own container returns a string.**

## Important

- **Every action result value sets every field.** A `single` value sets `copyable`, `qr`, `masked` and `launchable`; a `multiline` value sets `copyable`, `qr`, `masked` and `filename` (`null` for no download); a group member sets `description` (`null` for none). Add `launchable: false` wherever a result has none.

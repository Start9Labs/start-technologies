**start-cli 2.5.2 packs container images exactly as their layers provide them.**

## Highlights

- `s9pk pack` keeps an image's `/etc/resolv.conf` as the image provides it.

## Important

- A service whose image's `/etc/resolv.conf` is a symlink, such as a Nix-built image, must use start-sdk 3.1.1 or later before it is repacked with this version.

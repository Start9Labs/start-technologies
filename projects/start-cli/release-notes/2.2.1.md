**start-cli 2.2.1 simplifies service packaging with built-in SquashFS support.**

## Highlights

- **Pack services and add container images with built-in SquashFS support.** `s9pk pack` and `s9pk edit add-image` create compressed images directly. Packaged JavaScript files are readable and their directories traversable by the service runtime, while your source permissions stay unchanged.
- **Package manifest paths containing apostrophes work during packing and validation.**

**start-cli 2.6.0 adds automatic-backup administration and exits quietly when a shell pipeline closes its output.**

## Highlights

- Create, inspect, pause, resume, and remove automatic-backup jobs, or run a job immediately.
- Inspect backup activity, capacity estimates, checkpoint history, and retention previews; apply retention policies or restore selected service checkpoints and System data.
- Commands stop quietly when their output pipe closes, such as when piping output through `head`.

## Important

- Automatic-backup commands require a StartOS version that supports scheduled backups.
- Applying retention or deleting checkpoints permanently removes the selected backup data. Review the retention preview before applying it.

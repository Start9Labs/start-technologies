- Manage backups from a single System → Backups page: add local folders, external drives and CIFS shares as backup targets, see each target's status, last backup and free space, create and restore backups, and review past backups with their reports in a paginated history. Saved CIFS targets retain their connection details and IDs. Removing a local target deletes its folder and backups; external-drive and CIFS contents are preserved.

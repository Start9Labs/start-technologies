## ⚠️ Before You Update

> **Server Pure:** the first start after this update also updates your server's firmware. Your server will be offline for up to 5 minutes. **Do not unplug or power it off during this time.** An interrupted firmware update can leave it unable to start.
>
> Progress is shown only at `http://<your-server>.local` on your local network (`http`, not `https`). Over `https`, a public domain or Tor, the server is unreachable until the firmware update finishes. When your server is back, a notification confirms the result.

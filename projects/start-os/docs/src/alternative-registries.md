# Alternative Registries

Beyond the [default registries](default-registries.md), you can add alternative registries to your Marketplace. Custom registries are useful for organizations packaging services for their own users, developers distributing services outside the default registries, or communities curating specialized collections. To switch between registries or add new ones, click "Switch" beneath the current registry title in the sidebar.

Adding a registry offers two choices: pick a verified registry from the list Start9 publishes, or enter a registry's URL yourself.

## Verified Registries

Start9 publishes a list of registries it has verified — its own, and others whose operators it knows — with the name, icon, and description each one shows and a notice about it. A verified registry carries a **Verified by Start9** mark beside its name, names its operator with a contact email, and the marketplace shows the values Start9 published for it rather than what the registry reports. While one is selected, its notice appears in a banner at the top of the marketplace. A registry that is not on the list cannot present a verified registry's name — including any of Start9's own; StartOS shows it by its address instead and says so.

> [!NOTE]
> Verification is not an endorsement. Start9 does not operate, vet, or support verified registries other than its own, and each listing's notice says so. To have a registry of your own verified, see [Verification](/packaging/host-registry-verification.html) in the packaging guide.

## Custom Registries

Any registry reachable by URL can be added by hand, whether or not it is verified, including one reachable only over Tor. Nothing is required of it. A banner at the top of the marketplace says it is not on Start9's list while it is selected.

> [!NOTE]
> Start9 does not vet, endorse, or support services from custom registries. Exercise caution and only add registries you trust.

## Hosting an Alternative Registry

Install the "StartOS Registry" service from the Marketplace and follow instructions.

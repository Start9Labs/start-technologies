# Dux

Every chat starts with Dux, Start9's AI support agent, and a person from Start9 joins when one is needed. This page covers what Dux works from, answering its questions, how to reach a person, and what you can see of the work on your case.

## What Dux Works From

Dux answers first, at any hour. It reads what you write and what you attach: logs, archives of logs, PDFs and screenshots. It works from Start9's documentation, every service's own documentation, the issues and pull requests in Start9's public repositories, and the source code of Start9's software and of the software a service runs, at the version you run. It asks questions when the answer depends on something it hasn't been told.

While Dux works on a reply, the chat shows its latest steps. Under each reply, **How Dux worked this out** opens the research behind it: what it searched and read, and what it made of it.

When your case shows that something of Start9's is wrong, such as a bug or a gap in the documentation, Dux files a report with Start9 on its own. It files a feature request when you want a product or service to do something it doesn't do yet, once you ask it to or agree. A security problem is never reported publicly; Dux brings in a person instead.

## Answering Dux's Questions

When Dux needs something from you, its questions wait on a card above the message field, **Questions from Dux**. On a phone, tap the bar to open them.

Each question has a button along the top, with a checked box once you've answered it. Pick an answer or write one, and move between questions with **Back** and **Next**, or the arrow keys on a keyboard. Choosing an option you already picked clears it. **Review** shows everything you've answered, and sending posts the answers together as one message.

You don't have to answer every question at once. One you skip stays on the card, and Dux won't assume its answer. You can also answer in an ordinary message, and Dux takes a question off the card once it no longer matters.

## Reaching a Person

Choose **Request a human** from **Actions**, the **…** in the message field, or ask Dux for a person. Dux also brings one in when a case needs it, and hands them a summary of what it has established so far, so you don't have to start over.

During support hours, someone from the team joins shortly. When Dux hands a case over outside support hours, its reply says when they resume. Add anything that would help while you wait — error messages, logs, what you have already tried — and it will be waiting for whoever picks it up. Dux keeps helping while you wait.

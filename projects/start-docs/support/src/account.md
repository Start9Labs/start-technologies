# Your Account

A Start9 Support account is created with an access code, and is yours for life. This page covers where to find your code, creating the account, getting back in when you forget your password, and deleting the account.

## Access Codes

An access code creates one Start9 Support account. Every Start9 server and router comes with one, and so does every Start9 Support membership — a lifetime membership for anyone running StartOS, on any hardware, sold at [store.start9.com](https://store.start9.com/products/start9-support).

- **On your server** — servers from before February 2025, and servers bought from a reseller, carry the code on a label on the bottom.
- **In your email** — orders from Start9 since then get it by email, sent to the address the order was placed with.

If you can't find it, choose **What is an access code, and where do I find mine?** on the sign-up page, enter your order number and the email you ordered with, and any unused access code on that order is emailed to that address. If nothing arrives, or the code was already used, email your order number to [support@start9.com](mailto:support@start9.com).

## Creating an Account

1. Open [support.start9.com](https://support.start9.com) and choose **Create an account**.
1. Enter your access code and your email, and choose **Email me a sign-up link**.
1. Open the link in the email, choose a name and a password of at least 12 characters, and choose **Create account**.

Creating an account means agreeing to the [Terms of Service](https://support.start9.com/terms) and the [Privacy Policy](https://support.start9.com/privacy). A short welcome follows the first sign-in.

## Signing In and Out

Sign in with your email and password. **Sign out** is in the account menu, which opens from your name at the bottom of the chat list.

## Forgot Your Password

Choose **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page, enter your email, and open the link that arrives to choose a new password.

An emailed link works once and expires. If a link says it has expired, was already used or is not valid, request a new one from the same page.

## Email

Start9 Support emails you only about your account: sign-up and password links, and a recovered access code. Chats are never emailed.

## Deleting Your Account

Choose **Delete account** in **Settings**, under **Security**, and enter your password.

- **At once** — you are signed out everywhere, your chats close, and other customers can no longer read them.
- **30 days later** — your account is deleted for good, with your chats, files and private threads, unless the law requires Start9 to keep them. The [Privacy Policy](https://support.start9.com/privacy) says when that can be.

Your access code cannot be used again, so coming back means a new code. To keep a copy of a chat, [export it](chats.md#exporting-a-chat) before you delete the account.

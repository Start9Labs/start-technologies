# Email

Start9 Support emails you about your chats in two ways, both on by default: a reminder when a reply goes unread, and a transcript when a chat closes. This page covers both and how to turn them off.

## Unread-Reply Reminders

When a reply from Start9 has gone unread for a while, you are emailed a reminder. **Settings** says how long it waits. The reminder links to the chat; reply there, in Start9 Support.

## Chat Transcripts

When a chat closes, you are emailed a copy of it: every message with its author and time, and the names of its attachments. Private notes and attachments appear by label only.

## Turning Them Off

Each is a switch under **Email** in **Settings**: **Unread reply reminders** and **Chat transcripts**. Every reminder and transcript also carries a link that turns that kind of email off without signing in.

Emails about your account — sign-up and password links, a recovered access code — are always sent.

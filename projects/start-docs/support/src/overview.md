# Overview

Start9 Support is the app for Premium Support. Use it at support.start9.com, or [install it as an app](installing-the-app.md) on your phone or computer.

- Your [account](account.md) is created with an access code. Only Start9 sees your email address and access code; everyone else sees the display name you choose.
- Every request for help is a [chat](chats.md), opened under one topic. Every Start9 Support customer can read a chat, under its customer's display name and with secrets masked, unless Start9 has hidden it. Another customer's chat is read-only to you.
- [Dux](dux-and-helix.md), Start9's AI support agent, answers first, at any hour. You can choose Request a human, or ask Dux for a person, and a person from Start9 joins during support hours.
- Dux's questions appear on a card above your message field, where you can answer them.
- When Helix, Start9's engineering agent, investigates your case, an Investigation view in the chat's header shows what Dux and Helix have said to each other.
- For something only Start9's staff should see, such as an order number or an address, a person from Start9 opens the chat's [private thread](privacy.md).
- A closed chat can't be written in. You continue it in a new chat, which is linked to the old one and starts from Dux's notes on the case. An inactive chat closes on its own, but never while you're waiting on a person or Helix is investigating.

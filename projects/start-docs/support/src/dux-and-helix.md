# Dux and Helix

Every chat starts with Dux, Start9's AI support technician, and a person from Start9 joins when one is needed. This page covers who answers, how to reach a person, and what you can see of the work on your case.

## Dux

Dux answers first, at any hour. It works from Start9's documentation, every service's own documentation, and the issues and pull requests in Start9's public repositories, and it asks questions when the answer depends on something it hasn't been told.

Under each of Dux's replies, **How Dux worked this out** opens the research behind it: what it searched and read, and what it made of it. **Dux's notes**, in **Chat info**, are its running summary of the case — what is established, what has been ruled out, what is still open — kept up to date as the chat goes on.

Dux can also file a feature request with Start9 when you want a product or service to do something it doesn't do yet, once you ask it to or agree.

## Reaching a Person

Choose **Request a human**, the person icon in the message field, or ask Dux for a person. Dux also brings one in when a case needs it.

During support hours, someone from the team joins shortly. When Dux hands a case over outside support hours, its reply says when they resume. Add anything that would help while you wait — error messages, logs, what you have already tried — and it will be waiting for whoever picks it up. Dux keeps helping while you wait.

## Helix

When Dux can't find an answer and the evidence points at Start9's code, it asks Helix, Start9's engineering agent, to investigate. Helix reads the source code of the product or service, and may file a bug report. If Helix can't settle it, a person from the team takes over.

Once Helix has been asked to look at your case, an **Investigation** icon appears in the chat's header. It shows what Dux and Helix have said to each other, including any report Helix filed. Dux tells you what Helix found once it has an answer.

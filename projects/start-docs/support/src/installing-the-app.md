# Installing the App

Start9 Support installs as an app on your phone or computer, with its own icon and window. On iPhone and iPad, the installed app is the only place notifications work.

## Install It

{{#tabs global="platform"}}
{{#tab name="iOS"}}

1. Open [support.start9.com](https://support.start9.com) in Safari.
1. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
1. Open **Support** from your Home Screen, and sign in there.

{{#endtab}}
{{#tab name="Android / Graphene"}}

1. Open [support.start9.com](https://support.start9.com) in your browser.
1. Open the browser's menu and choose **Install app** (some browsers call it **Add to Home screen**).
1. Open **Support** from your app list.

{{#endtab}}
{{#tab name="Mac"}}

In Chrome or Edge, open [support.start9.com](https://support.start9.com) and choose the install icon in the address bar. In Safari, choose **File**, then **Add to Dock**.

{{#endtab}}
{{#tab name="Windows"}}

In Chrome or Edge, open [support.start9.com](https://support.start9.com) and choose the install icon in the address bar.

{{#endtab}}
{{#tab name="Linux"}}

In Chrome or Chromium, open [support.start9.com](https://support.start9.com) and choose the install icon in the address bar. Otherwise, use Start9 Support in a browser tab.

{{#endtab}}
{{#endtabs}}

## Updates

The app updates itself. When a new version is ready, a **Reload** message appears; choose it when it suits you, since a message you are still typing is lost on reload.
